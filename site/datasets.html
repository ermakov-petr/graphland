web-fraud, web-topics, and web-traffic

These datasets share a web graph. Nodes are websites; a directed edge records that at least one user followed a link from one website to another during a selected period. The tasks are fraudulent-website detection in web-fraud (strongly imbalanced binary classification), website-topic prediction in web-topics (multiclass classification), and visitor-count prediction in web-traffic (regression).

web-fraud · APweb-topics · Accuracyweb-traffic · R²

artnet-views and artnet-exp

These datasets share a social graph from Shedevrum, a generative AI art platform. Nodes are users, and edges connect friends. The tasks are predicting the number of views a user receives during a selected period in artnet-views (regression), and identifying users who tend to create explicit art content in artnet-exp (binary classification).

artnet-exp · APartnet-views · R²

city-roads-M and city-roads-L

These datasets describe road networks in two major cities. Nodes are road segments. A directed edge connects adjacent segments when traffic rules permit movement from one to the other. The task is to predict a segment’s average travel speed at a specified timestamp (regression).

city-roads-M · R²city-roads-L · R²

city-reviews

This dataset describes a place-review service in two major cities. Nodes are users who rate places and organizations; an edge connects users who often review the same organizations. The task is to identify users who leave fraudulent reviews (binary classification).

city-reviews · AP

avazu-ctr

This dataset is based on Avazu advertising-interaction data. Nodes are devices used to access the Internet, and an edge connects devices that often visit the same websites. The task is to predict advertisement click-through rate for each device (regression).

avazu-ctr · R²

hm-categories and hm-prices

These datasets share a product co-purchasing graph from H&M. Nodes are products, and an edge connects products often bought by the same customers. The tasks are product-category prediction in hm-categories (multiclass classification) and product-price prediction in hm-prices (regression).

hm-categories · Accuracyhm-prices · R²

pokec-regions

This dataset describes the Pokec online social network. Nodes are users, and a directed edge indicates that one user marked another as a friend. The task is to predict a user’s region, an extreme multiclass classification problem with 183 classes.

pokec-regions · Accuracy

twitch-views

This dataset describes the Twitch live-streaming network. Nodes are users, and an edge connects two users who follow each other. The task is to predict how many views a user receives during a selected period (regression).

twitch-views · R²

tolokers-2

This dataset describes workers on the Toloka crowdsourcing platform. Nodes are workers, and an edge connects workers who have completed the same task. The task is to predict which workers were banned in one of the projects (binary classification).

tolokers-2 · AP
